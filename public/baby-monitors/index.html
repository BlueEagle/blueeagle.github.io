For a bigger screen, the VAVA 8-inch is the sharpest. The VTech HQ Max is the value pick.

Three of these four are closed-loop, non-Wi-Fi systems: the camera talks only to its own parent unit over 2.4 GHz radio, with no app, account or cloud. That removes the attack surface behind nearly every real baby-monitor hack. The fourth, the Babysense touchscreen, has Wi-Fi built in but can run in a local mode with it switched off. Bigger screens mostly mean softer pictures, so the real trade-off is size against sharpness.

Why cloud monitors keep failing

In May 2026, researcher Sammy Azdoufal found flaws in Meari's shared backend, which powers Amazon brands like Arenti, Boifun and ieGeek. More than a million cameras were affected. One bug, CVE-2026-33359, left motion-alert images on Alibaba OSS with no auth or expiry. Another used a hardcoded signing key that ships in every Meari-based app. One backend bug hits every customer at once.

Where "hack-proof" is marketing

FHSS is frequency hopping, not encryption. None of these vendors publish their cipher, key exchange or pairing protocol. A neighbor with a wideband SDR and patience could in principle reverse-engineer a link. Researchers did that to Chamberlain's FHSS garage sensor, and cheap analog 2.4 GHz cameras have been sniffed from a passing car. Still, a targeted RF attack on your street is a far smaller threat model than a bulk cloud breach.

Setup checklist for any of them

  • Buy new and sealed, not open-box or used. Then pair the camera to the parent unit yourself, so no unknown unit is linked.
  • Check the camera list on the parent unit now and then. Only your own cameras should show.
  • Mount the camera at least 3 ft from the crib and keep cords out of reach. That's standard AAP/CPSC guidance and matters more than any radio threat.
  • With the Babysense touchscreen, leave Wi-Fi off unless you need the app. If you turn it on, give it its own IoT network and turn it back off when you're done.
  • Remember the camera has two-way talk. It transmits audio whenever talk-back is on, so keep that off when you aren't using it.
  • If you later want phone viewing, skip vendor clouds. Use a PoE or RTSP camera on an isolated IoT VLAN, record locally (Frigate or similar) and reach it over WireGuard. That way you own every hop.
