Radio lingo, decoded

Ham radio
Licensed amateur radio. Hobbyists talk locally and worldwide, and they help with communication when phones and internet go down.
HF
"High Frequency" bands that can reach across countries and oceans.
Dual band
Works on the two most common local ham bands, called 2 meters and 70 centimeters.
BNC
A plug style for connecting antennas. The links above already match my radios.
Fox hunting
A treasure hunt where you track down a hidden transmitter by following its signal.
